# Surface brief: index.html (home)

Scope: the single-page marketing site. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: SME owners in the US and LatAm evaluating, often on a phone. Action: build a plan and send it on WhatsApp, or write in.
Constraints: keep current copy, ES/EN toggle, "Arma tu plan". No invented proof. Contact data still DGP placeholders.
