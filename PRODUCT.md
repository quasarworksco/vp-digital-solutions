# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and managers of small and medium businesses (pymes) in the United States and Latin America, with no particular ethnic or niche focus. They run service businesses (home services, construction, health and wellness, online stores, professional services, real estate, automotive and transport, schools and courses, startups) and are stretched thin: leads are lost in chats and spreadsheets, messages go unanswered, the website is weak or missing, social media is abandoned, and they coordinate several unconnected vendors. They arrive to understand what VP Digital Solutions can take off their hands and to request a proposal.

## Product Purpose

VP Digital Solutions is a single bilingual (Spanish/English) team that designs, builds and operates a company's whole digital side: online presence, internal systems and remote staff. The website exists to explain that offer clearly and turn visits into proposal requests (via the plan builder, WhatsApp or email). Success means a qualified business owner sends a request with the modules they need.

## Positioning

One team responsible for everything digital, in both languages, instead of five unconnected providers. The pieces are built to connect (website feeds the CRM, the CRM feeds the AI assistant, the team sees everything), the client owns what is built (custom CRM with no per-user license fees), and AI handles the repetitive work while real people handle what matters.

## Operating Context

- Offer organized in three pillars: Presence, Systems, Team.
- Engagement process: free diagnosis, proposal with scope, clear price and dates, staged build with client approval at each stage, launch with team training and ongoing support.
- Visitors evaluate on phones as much as on desktop; contact happens mainly through WhatsApp.
- Served markets: US, Latin America and Europe; content in Spanish and English.

## Capabilities and Constraints

All services currently listed on the site are confirmed real:
- Premium websites (landing, multi-page, e-commerce, bookings) and online stores
- Custom CRM (clients, sales pipeline, calendar, tasks, payments, team permissions; connected to web, WhatsApp, calls and SMS; one-time payment, owned by the client)
- AI assistants 24/7 (WhatsApp, web chat, phone; answer, qualify and book)
- Bilingual human virtual assistants (remote, flexible hourly plans)
- Call center and customer service
- Automations and integrations (APIs, payments, email)
- Social media management and monthly content (Facebook, Instagram)
- SOPs and documented processes
- Team training platform by levels and roles, with certifications and a manager panel
- Brand and visual identity
- SEO and Google Business

Technical constraints:
- Static single-page site in `index.html` (CSS and JS inline) with images in `assets/`, published by GitHub Pages at `vpdigitalsolutions.3s-dev.com`.
- Bilingual ES/EN with a language toggle.
- Delivery page `/entrega` is planned for handoff (noindex, excluded from sitemap and menu).

Open decisions:
- **Contact data:** VP Digital Solutions' own email, phone/WhatsApp, address, hours and social profiles are not defined yet. The site keeps DGP Global Group's details as placeholders (WhatsApp +1 (239) 823-1738, info@dgpglobalgroup.com) until the client provides them. Do not invent others.
- **Official logo:** not supplied; the current monogram SVG is provisional.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: VP Digital Solutions.
- Voice: plain, direct and confident, written to a business owner ("tú"), focused on outcomes (more clients, less chaos, order and control).
- Visual direction (chosen by the client, Nov 2026): classic premium agency layout on a light background, with the brand blues (#0b3bb5, #1450e6, sky #3d8bfd, teal #0fa3a3 on navy #0a1a33) and the square-ish sans fonts Plus Jakarta Sans (headings) and Inter (text). The client explicitly rejected gold and serif type.
- Must keep: bilingual ES/EN toggle, the "Arma tu plan" builder, and the current copy.
- No emojis; SVG icons only.
- Footer credits "Diseñado por DGP Global Group" linking to https://dgpglobalgroup.com.

## Evidence on Hand

- Photography in `assets/img/` (hero, meeting, team, workshop, training, strategy, service, web, CRM, AI, social) and `assets/og-image.jpg`.
- No testimonials, client logos, case studies or metrics exist. The site must not show testimonials, client counts, results figures or customer names until real ones are supplied. Interface mockups (for example sample CRM numbers) must read as illustrative product UI, not claims.

## Product Principles

1. One accountable team: every section should reinforce that presence, systems and people come from one place and connect.
2. Bilingual parity: Spanish and English carry the same depth and quality; neither is a translation afterthought.
3. Ownership and clarity: clear pricing up front, staged delivery, and assets that belong to the client.
4. Technology plus people: AI does the repetitive work; humans stay visible and in charge.
5. Honesty over hype: no invented proof; claims stay concrete and verifiable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Spanish and English audiences with equal access; mobile-first with large tap targets; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; WCAG AA contrast as the baseline.
